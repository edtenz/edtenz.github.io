THREE WORDS YOU NEED TODAY

Three Concepts, One Question

🏷️

Identity Label

身份标签
A shortcut for who you are.

Any category you use to define yourself or others: MBTI type, zodiac sign, generation, nationality, job title, gender role.

📦

Stereotype

刻板印象
A label that becomes a box.

When a label hardens into an expectation. "Engineers can't communicate." "Scorpios are vengeful." The shortcut becomes a prison.

🔄

Self-Fulfilling Prophecy

自我实现预言
The label shapes you back.

When you start behaving according to a label because you believe it. You say "I'm an introvert" — then stop trying to connect.

Today's question: Do labels reveal who we are — or do they decide who we become?

A CLASSIC TRICK

The Barnum Effect: Why every label "feels right"

In 1948, psychologist Bertram Forer gave students a "personalized" personality assessment. They rated its accuracy 4.3 out of 5. The catch? Everyone got the exact same text:

THE "PERSONALIZED" RESULT
"You have a need for other people to like and admire you, yet you tend to be critical of yourself. While you have some personality weaknesses, you are generally able to compensate（弥补） for them."
Every single student thought this was written specifically for them.
WHY IT MATTERS
"You are an independent thinker who doesn't accept others' statements without proof — but sometimes you are extroverted, sociable, while at other times you are introverted and reserved."
This is how MBTI descriptions, horoscopes, and zodiac profiles work. Vague enough to fit anyone.

The question isn't whether your label is accurate. It's why you need one at all.

ROUND 1 · 4 QUESTIONS · 25 MIN

The Labels We Wear

Q1
Your Label Collection

What labels do you currently use to describe yourself? (MBTI, zodiac, generation, job, hometown…) Which one do you use most often, and why?

Q2
The Label That Didn't Fit

Has someone ever labeled you with something that felt completely wrong? How did you react — did you push back, or quietly accept it?

Q3
Comfort vs. Cage

Think of a label you genuinely enjoy (like your MBTI). Has it ever stopped you from trying something? ("I'm an introvert, so I won't go to that party.")

Q4
Labeling Others

Be honest: have you ever judged someone based on their zodiac sign, hometown, or job title — before actually knowing them? What happened?

Take turns — each person answers Q1 before moving on. About 6 minutes per question.

BEFORE ROUND 2 · THE STAKES

Labels aren't just personality quizzes.

When identity labels harden into systems, real consequences follow.

🧠

Stereotype Threat（刻板印象威胁）

Stanford research (Claude Steele, 1995): when people are reminded of a negative stereotype about their group, their performance drops — even when they don't believe the stereotype.

💼

Hiring Bias（招聘偏见）

A 2023 study found that Chinese companies screening by zodiac sign or MBTI rejected qualified candidates. Some job posts explicitly say "no Virgos" or "INTJs only."

🌏

Regional Discrimination

地域歧视: In China, people from certain provinces face prejudice in dating and hiring — based entirely on their hukou label, not their character.

Sources: Steele & Aronson (1995), South China Morning Post (2023), China Labour Bulletin

ROUND 2 · QUESTION 1

Do Labels Help or Harm?

Some people say labels give us language to understand ourselves ("learning I'm ADHD changed my life"). Others say they trap us in boxes. Is labeling a net positive or negative（总体利大于弊还是弊大于利） for society?

🏷️ LABELS HELP US

  • They give language to feelings we couldn't name
  • They build community ("finding my people")
  • They can be tools for self-understanding
  • Medical labels (ADHD, autism) unlock support

📦 LABELS LIMIT US

  • They reduce complex humans to categories
  • They create in-groups and out-groups（内群体和外群体）
  • They become excuses ("that's just how Scorpios are")
  • They make us stop growing ("I'm not a ___ person")

Discussion tip: Think of a specific label that has BOTH helped and limited you.

ROUND 2 · QUESTION 2 · GROUP EXPLORATION

How are labels formed?

Labels don't come from nowhere. Split into 4 mini-groups — each explores one force that shapes labels. 5 min prep, 10 min cross-discussion.

📱

MEDIA & PLATFORMS

(Algorithms, content, trends)

Platforms sort us into categories for engagement — MBTI filters, zodiac tags, "你是哪种人" quizzes. How do algorithms reinforce labels?

👥

SOCIAL CIRCLES

(Friends, family, community)

We label each other to build belonging — "you're the funny one," "she's the smart one." How does your group shape your identity?

💰

COMMERCIAL INTERESTS

(Brands, marketers, industries)

Companies segment us into labels to sell to us — "Gen-Z style," "INTJ gift guide," "Scorpio candle." How does commerce manufacture identity?

🧠

HUMAN PSYCHOLOGY

(Cognitive shortcuts（认知捷径）, survival instincts)

Our brains crave categories to reduce complexity — it's how we survived. Is labeling simply hardwired, or can we override it?

Key insight: labels aren't anyone's "fault" — they emerge from systems. But which force shapes YOU most?

ROUND 2 · QUESTION 3 · CLOSING

Living beyond the label.

What's ONE thing you could do this week to see yourself — or someone else — beyond a label?
