THREE WORDS YOU NEED TODAY

Three Styles, One Debate

📸

Check-in Tourism

打卡式旅行
See it, snap it, leave.

Travel as a checklist: visit the famous spots, take the photo, prove you were there. The destination is content, not experience.

🏃

Speed Tourism

特种兵旅游
Maximum places, minimum time.

A Gen-Z trend: overnight trains, 5am starts, 10 attractions in 48 hours. Exhausting but "efficient." Travel as endurance sport.

🐌

Slow Travel

深度旅行
Be there, not just pass through.

Stay longer, see less, feel more. Cook local food, learn a few words, get lost on purpose. Travel as transformation, not transaction.

No judgment — all three are valid. But today we ask: what are you really looking for when you travel?

THE REAL QUESTION

Are you traveling for yourself — or for your audience?

A 2023 study from the University of Southern California found:

📱 When you photograph an experience

Visual memory improves slightly
But emotional memory（情感记忆）decreases
Enjoyment drops by ~15% in the moment
You remember what it looked like, not how it felt

👁️ When you just experience it

You are more emotionally present
You form richer, sensory memories（感官记忆）
You report higher satisfaction afterward
You remember how it felt, not just how it looked

The camera becomes a wall between you and the moment. But does that mean we should stop taking photos?

BEFORE ROUND 2 · THE BIGGER PICTURE

Travel as a mirror of our times.

How we travel reveals what we value — and what we're running from.

💰

The Experience Economy（体验经济）

We spend more on experiences than objects — but then reduce experiences to shareable content. Are we buying memories or buying proof?

😰

FOMO Tourism（跟风旅游）

"Everyone's been to ___, so I have to go too." Travel driven by social comparison, not genuine curiosity. The anxiety of missing out becomes the trip itself.

🏃

Burnout Escapes（逃离倦怠）

Many young Chinese say they travel to "escape 996 culture" — but then fill their trips with the same productivity mindset: more, faster, optimized.

Sources: Pine & Gilmore "Experience Economy" (2011), Deloitte Travel Survey 2024, China Youth Daily

ROUND 2 · 25 MIN

The Deeper
Questions

3 questions · switch groups · go deeper

ROUND 2 · QUESTION 1

Relaxation or Performance?

Be honest: when you travel, are you doing it for yourself — or partly to show others that you have an interesting life? Is there anything wrong with traveling "for the gram"?

📸 POSTING IS FINE

  • Sharing is a natural way to process joy
  • Photos help you remember the trip later
  • Inspiring others to travel is a good thing
  • You can enjoy something AND photograph it

👁️ IT CHANGES THE EXPERIENCE

  • You start choosing destinations for their "shareability（出片率/可分享性）"
  • You experience the moment through a screen
  • The trip becomes labor — angles, captions, edits
  • If you didn't post it, would you still have gone?

Key question: If social media disappeared tomorrow, would your travel style change?

ROUND 2 · QUESTION 2 · GROUP EXPLORATION

What shapes how we travel?

Our travel style isn't random — it's shaped by invisible forces. Split into 4 groups, each explores one force. 5 min prep, 10 min cross-discussion.

📱

SOCIAL MEDIA

(Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Instagram)

Platforms reward photogenic moments over meaningful ones. How do algorithms shape where we go and what we do there?

💼

WORK CULTURE

(996, limited vacation, burnout)

When you only have 3 days off, speed tourism is rational. Does overwork force us into shallow travel?

👥

PEER PRESSURE

(FOMO, status, comparison)

"Everyone in my circle has been to Japan." Travel as social currency（社交货币）— when did it become competitive?

🧠

INNER NEEDS

(Escape, growth, curiosity)

Some travel to run away, some to find something. What's the emotional engine behind your last trip?

No force is good or bad — but awareness changes behavior. Which one drives YOU most?
