# Host Script: Productivity Culture

> **效率崇拜：我们是在管理时间，还是被时间管理了？**
> 90 min · 2 rounds · 7 questions · 6 open cards

---

## Pre-Session Checklist / 开场前准备

- [ ] Test slides, projector, clicker / 测试投影和翻页器
- [ ] Arrange seats in groups of 5-7 / 分组就座
- [ ] Prepare timer (visible to you) / 准备计时器
- [ ] Arrive 10 min early, greet people at the door / 提前到场

---

## Slide 1 — Title (1 min)

**What to do:** Welcome everyone. Read the title with energy.

> "Welcome to English Corner! Today's topic: **Productivity Culture** — To-Do Lists, Streaks, Self-Discipline, and the Anxiety In Between."

> "效率崇拜——我们真的在管理时间，还是已经被时间管理了？"

**中文提示：** 今天话题贴近每个人，很容易引发共鸣，开场可以轻松一些。

---

## Slide 2 — Ice Breaker (5 min)

**What to do:** Pair people up. Demo your own answer first.

> "Turn to the person next to you. Share: your name, what you do, and your morning routine. Be honest — structured morning, chaotic morning, or 'I hit snooze five times' morning."

**Demo:** "I'm Eddie. I work as ___. The first thing I do every morning is check my phone. It makes me feel... immediately behind."

**Timing:** 1 min per person. At 4 min: "30 seconds to wrap up!"

---

## Slide 3 — Warm-up: 30-Second Experiment (3 min)

**What to do:** Lead a micro-experiment. Everyone does nothing for 30 seconds, then reflects.

> "Put your phone face-down. Close your eyes. I'm going to time 30 seconds of doing **absolutely nothing**. No planning, no thinking about your to-do list. Just breathe. Ready? Go."

**Time 30 seconds in silence.**

> "OK, open your eyes. Raise your hand if you felt uncomfortable, restless, or guilty during those 30 seconds."

**Pause — let people look around.**

> "Now — one word for how that felt. Volunteers? 'Peaceful'? 'Anxious'? 'Weird'?"

**Take 2-3 answers.**

> "If 30 seconds of nothing felt hard — that is exactly what today is about."

**中文提示：** 这个实验效果非常好。大部分人会举手说不舒服——这就是进入话题的最佳方式。关键是30秒要完全安静，主持人也不要说话。

---

## Slide 4 — Three Concepts (3 min)

**What to do:** Teach the three terms clearly.

> "Three words for today:"

1. **Productivity Culture** (效率崇拜) — "A belief that your worth is measured by your output."
2. **Hustle Mentality** (自律打卡) — "Tracking streaks, waking early, posting progress — life as a performance dashboard."
3. **Intentional Rest** (有意识的休息) — "Choosing to stop not because you're exhausted, but because rest itself has value."

> "Core question: **Are we managing time, or is 'being productive' managing us?**"

---

## Slide 5 — Stats (2 min)

**What to do:** Read stats with dramatic pauses.

> - "Average weekly working hours in China: **49 hours.**"
> - "**77%** of professionals globally have experienced burnout."
> - "China's 'knowledge payment' industry: **¥259 billion** in 2024."
> - "**480 million** users of self-improvement content on Chinese platforms."

> "When 'not being productive' feels like falling behind, rest becomes another task to optimize."

---

## Slide 6 — Four Perspectives (2 min)

**What to do:** Walk through quickly. Plant seeds for discussion.

> "Four things hidden in these numbers:"
> 1. "**Real Improvement** — apps and trackers genuinely help people build skills."
> 2. "**Performance Pressure** — sharing routines on social media turns discipline into spectacle."
> 3. "**Guilt Economy** — apps use streaks and loss aversion to make you feel bad when you stop."
> 4. "**Health Cost** — burnout, anxiety, and sleep deprivation are rising."

---

## Slide 7 — Round 1 Title (30 sec)

> "Round 1: **Your Relationship With Productivity.** 25 minutes, 4 questions. Small groups. Start from real experience."

---

## Slide 8 — Round 1 Questions (25 min)

**Read all 4 questions, then let groups discuss:**

- **Q1 The To-Do List Test:** "How do you feel when your to-do list isn't finished? Guilty, anxious, relieved, or indifferent?"
- **Q2 Streaks and Tracking:** "Do you use apps to track habits? What happens emotionally when you break a streak?"
- **Q3 The Weekend Dilemma:** "When you have a free day, do you feel peace or pressure? What counts as 'wasted'?"
- **Q4 The Comparison Scroll:** "Have you seen someone's morning routine online and felt you were 'falling behind'?"

> "Everyone answers Q1 first, then move deeper. About 6 minutes per question."

**Facilitation:**
- Walk between groups. If a group is silent, join them and share YOUR answer.
- If someone dominates: "Great insight! Who else has a different experience?"
- At 20 min: "Two more minutes, then we'll move on."

---

## Slide 9 — Useful Expressions (shown during Round 1)

> "Need phrases? Try these: 'I feel guilty when I ___', 'I put pressure on myself to ___', 'The line between discipline and anxiety is ___'."

---

## Slide 10 — Transition (2 min)

> "Great discussions. Before Round 2, notice: productivity can be three things at the same time:"
> 1. "A **Tool** — you choose when to use it."
> 2. "A **Performance** — others watch and compare."
> 3. "A **Control mechanism** — it promises certainty, but never quite delivers."

> "Round 2 is not about judging productivity. It's about seeing where it stops serving us."

---

## Slide 11 — Round 2 Title (30 sec)

> "Switch groups — find at least 2 new people. Round 2: **Questioning The System.** 3 deeper questions."

---

## Slide 12 — Round 2 Q1: Freedom or Cage? (8 min)

> "**Is self-discipline freedom or a new cage?**"

> "Left side: IT'S FREEDOM — discipline builds skills, structure reduces decision fatigue."
> "Right side: IT'S A CAGE — when rest requires justification, something is wrong. Streaks punish you for being human."

> "Start by defining what 'too much discipline' looks like for you personally."

**中文提示：** 如果全组同意一方，问："Can anyone argue the other side?"

---

## Slide 13 — Round 2 Q2: Origins (8 min)

> "**Where did 'you must be efficient' come from?** Four forces:"
> - "**Education** — scores, rankings, effort = grades = worth."
> - "**Family** — parents sacrificed, so resting feels like betrayal."
> - "**Social Media** — everyone's 5am routine makes 'normal' feel like failure."
> - "**Economic Pressure** — competitive job market, rising costs, slowing down feels like luxury."

> "Pick the force that shaped YOU the most. Ask: is it still useful, or just familiar?"

---

## Slide 14 — Round 2 Q3: Healthier Relationship (9 min)

> "**What would a healthier relationship with time look like?** Ideas to steal:"
> - "Schedule one 'unproductive' hour this week — protect it like a meeting."
> - "Ask: 'Am I doing this because I want to, or because I'll feel guilty if I don't?'"
> - "Turn off streak notifications for 7 days."
> - "Replace 'I wasted the day' with 'I rested today.'"
> - "Tell one person: 'I'm choosing to do less this week.'"

> "**Finish with: 'One thing I want to give myself permission to stop doing is ___.'"**

---

## Slide 15 — Six Open Cards (10 min)

> "Six cards, six questions. Tap one that calls to you — and answer honestly."

**Sample questions if needed verbally:**
- "What is one thing you do purely for fun, with zero goals?"
- "Who is the most relaxed person you know? What do they do differently?"
- "If your week had 8 days, would you use the extra day for work or rest?"

---

## Slide 16 — Closing Quote (2 min)

> "'Almost everything will work again if you unplug it for a few minutes, including you.' — Anne Lamott"

**Pause 3 seconds.**

> "Thanks for being here today — not to be productive, but to be present."
> "感谢你今天的真诚分享。允许自己慢下来。"

## Facilitation Cheat Sheet / 引导技巧速查

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| 冷场 | Join the group, share your answer first, then ask "What about you?" |
| 一人独占 | "Wonderful point! Let's hear from someone else." |
| 只说中文 | "Try saying just the key idea in English — even one sentence is great." |
| 跑题 | "Interesting — how does that connect to productivity?" |
| 能量下降 | Quick stretch or 30-second pair share to reset. |
| 超时 | Skip a Q from Round 1. Always protect Round 2 Q3 (action). |
| 有人情绪激动 | "That's real. Thank you for sharing." Pause. Move on gently. |
