# Host Script: Identity Labels

> **身份标签：它们帮助我们理解自己，还是限制了我们看待别人？**
> 90 min · 2 rounds · 7 questions · 8 blind box cards

---

## Pre-Session Checklist / 开场前准备

- [ ] Test slides and projector / 测试投影
- [ ] Arrange seats in groups of 5-7
- [ ] Prepare timer / 准备计时器
- [ ] Optional: print a few "fake" Barnum personality descriptions for Slide 6 activity

---

## Slide 1 — Title (1 min)

> "Welcome to English Corner! Tonight: **Who Are You Beyond Your Label?** — MBTI, Zodiac, Stereotypes, and The Stories We Tell."

> "身份标签——它们帮助我们理解自己，还是限制了我们看待别人？"

---

## Slide 2 — Ice Breaker (5 min)

**What to do:** This one is unique — ask people to share THREE labels they give themselves.

> "Pair up. Share: your name, what you do, and three labels you'd give yourself. For example: INTJ, Scorpio, night owl. Or: dog person, foodie, introvert."

**Demo:** "I'm Eddie. I'd label myself: ___, ___, and ___."

> "Notice: which labels did you reach for first? That says something."

---

## Slide 3 — Warm-up: Quick Poll (1 min)

**What to do:** Read each line. Ask people to raise hands. Comment on the results.

> "Raise your hand if..."
> - "You know your MBTI type." (大部分人会举手)
> - "You've checked someone's zodiac sign before dating."
> - "You've been told 'you don't seem like a ___'."
> - "You've judged someone based on their hometown."
> - "You've put your MBTI in your social media bio."

> "If most hands went up — we're all in the labeling game. Let's figure out why."

---

## Slide 4 — Three Concepts (3 min)

1. **Identity Label** (身份标签) — "A shortcut for who you are: MBTI, zodiac, job title, generation, hometown."
2. **Stereotype** (刻板印象) — "When a label hardens into an expectation. 'Engineers can't communicate.' The shortcut becomes a prison."
3. **Self-Fulfilling Prophecy** (自我实现预言) — "When you behave according to a label because you believe it. 'I'm an introvert, so I won't go.'"

> "Today's question: **Do labels reveal who we are — or decide who we become?**"

---

## Slide 5 — Stats (2 min)

> - "**2.5 million** people take the MBTI test every year."
> - "**88%** of Fortune 500 companies have used MBTI in hiring."
> - "**72%** of Chinese Gen-Z say they 'somewhat believe' in zodiac compatibility."
> - "But — **50%** of people who retake MBTI get a DIFFERENT type within 5 weeks."

> "We cling to labels as identity anchors — even when the science says they're unstable."

---

## Slide 6 — The Barnum Effect (3 min)

**What to do:** This is a key teaching moment. Read the "personalized" description aloud. Ask people if it sounds like them.

> "In 1948, psychologist Forer gave students a 'personalized' personality assessment. Everyone rated it 4.3 out of 5 for accuracy. The catch? Everyone got the EXACT same text."

**Read aloud:** "You have a need for other people to like you, yet you tend to be critical of yourself. While you have personality weaknesses, you are generally able to compensate for them."

> "Sound familiar? Sound like YOU? That's the Barnum Effect. It's how MBTI descriptions, horoscopes, and zodiac profiles work — vague enough to fit anyone."

> "The question isn't whether your label is accurate. It's **why you need one at all.**"

---

## Slide 7 — Round 1 Title (30 sec)

> "Round 1: **The Labels We Wear.** 25 minutes, 4 questions. Small groups. Share your real experience."

---

## Slide 8 — Round 1 Questions (25 min)

- **Q1 Your Label Collection:** "What labels do you use to describe yourself? Which one do you use most often, and why?"
- **Q2 The Label That Didn't Fit:** "Has someone labeled you with something that felt wrong? Did you push back or quietly accept it?"
- **Q3 Comfort vs. Cage:** "Has a label you enjoy ever stopped you from trying something?"
- **Q4 Labeling Others:** "Have you judged someone based on zodiac, hometown, or job title before knowing them?"

> "Everyone answers Q1 first, then move deeper."

**Facilitation tips:**
- This topic gets personal. Create safety: "No judgment in this room."
- If someone shares vulnerability, validate: "Thank you for being honest."
- If debate heats up: "Both can be true. Let's hear another perspective."

---

## Slide 9 — Useful Expressions (shown during Round 1)

> "Phrases to use: 'I identify as ___, but it doesn't define me', 'People assume I'm ___ because of ___', 'That label is reductive / limiting / empowering.'"

---

## Slide 10 — Stakes / Before Round 2 (2 min)

> "Labels aren't just personality quizzes. When they harden into systems, real consequences follow:"
> - "**Stereotype Threat:** Being reminded of a negative stereotype makes performance drop — even if you don't believe it."
> - "**Hiring Bias:** Some Chinese job posts say 'no Virgos' or 'INTJs only.'"
> - "**Regional Discrimination** (地域歧视): People from certain provinces face prejudice in dating and hiring."

---

## Slide 11 — Round 2 Title (30 sec)

> "Switch groups. Round 2: **The Bigger Picture.** 3 questions, deeper thinking."

---

## Slide 12 — Round 2 Q1: Help or Harm? (8 min)

> "**Do labels help or harm?**"
> - "HELP: They give language to feelings, build community, unlock support (ADHD diagnosis)."
> - "HARM: They reduce complex humans to categories, create in-groups, become excuses."

> "Think of a specific label that has BOTH helped and limited you."

---

## Slide 13 — Round 2 Q2: How Labels Form (8 min)

> "**How are labels formed?** Split into 4 forces:"
> - "**Media & Platforms** — algorithms sort us into categories for engagement."
> - "**Social Circles** — friends label us to build belonging."
> - "**Commercial Interests** — brands segment us to sell to us."
> - "**Human Psychology** — our brains crave categories to reduce complexity."

> "Which force shapes YOU most?"

---

## Slide 14 — Round 2 Q3: Living Beyond (9 min)

> "**What's ONE thing you could do this week to see yourself — or someone else — beyond a label?**"
> - "Ask someone about their life story, not their MBTI type."
> - "Try something your 'type' supposedly wouldn't do."
> - "Remove personality labels from your bio for a week."
> - "Notice next time you judge someone by job title or hometown."

> "**Finish with: 'This week, I will ___.'"** Say it out loud."

---

## Slide 15 — Blind Box Questions (10 min)

> "Eight boxes, eight questions. Tap one, answer in 2 minutes, invite someone else."

**If non-interactive:** Pick random cards and ask for volunteers.

---

## Slide 16 — Closing Quote (2 min)

> "'The single story creates stereotypes, and the problem with stereotypes is not that they are untrue, but that they are incomplete. They make one story become the only story.' — Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie"

> "You are not your label. You are the whole story."
> "你不是一个标签，你是一个完整的故事。"

## Facilitation Cheat Sheet

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| 冷场 | Share your own label story first. Vulnerability is contagious. |
| 一人独占 | "Fascinating! Who has a totally different experience?" |
| 话题敏感 | "This is a safe space. We're exploring, not judging." |
| 跑题 | "How does that connect to labels and identity?" |
| 能量下降 | Do a quick re-pair: "Turn to someone new, share one label you'd throw away." |
| 超时 | Skip Q4 in Round 1. Protect Round 2 Q3 (action). |
