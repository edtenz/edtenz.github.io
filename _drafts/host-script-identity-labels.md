# Host Script: Identity Labels

> **Labels We Live With**
> Identity Labels：它们帮助我们理解自己，还是限制我们看待别人？
> 90 min · 2 rounds · 14 slides · 6 flip cards

---

## Slide 1 — Title: Labels We Live With (2 min)

> "Welcome to English Corner. Today we are talking about **identity labels**: MBTI, zodiac signs, hometowns, jobs, ages, and all the quick words we use to understand people."

> "欢迎来到英语角。今天我们聊身份标签：MBTI、星座、家乡、职业、年龄，还有那些我们用来快速理解别人的词。"

> "The question is simple: do labels help us understand ourselves, or do they limit how we see people?"

> "今天的问题很简单：标签帮助我们理解自己，还是限制我们看待别人？"

**中文提示：** 语气保持轻松，不要一开场就批判 MBTI 或星座。可以补一句：“今天不是要证明标签好或坏，而是看看我们什么时候用它、为什么用它、它带来了什么影响。”

---

## Slide 2 — Self-Introduction: Name / Job / Label (6 min)

**What to do / 操作步骤：** 每人按屏幕上的三个提示做自我介绍：**Name / Job / Label**。其中 **Label** 部分只分享三个自己选择的标签。人数多时按小组进行；人数少时全场轮一遍。

> "Let's start with a quick check-in. On the screen, you can see three prompts: Name, Job, and Label. Please tell us what we should call you, what you do, and three labels you choose for yourself."

> "我们先做一个快速自我介绍。屏幕上有三个提示：Name、Job、Label。请告诉大家怎么称呼你、你现在做什么，以及三个你自己选择的标签。"

**Examples / 示例：**

- "I'm Eddie. I work in tech. My three labels are: tech person, coffee person, and chronic overthinker."
- "我是 ___。我做 ___。我的三个标签是：___、___、___。"
- "You can choose from personality, job, hometown, hobby, lifestyle, MBTI, zodiac, or a funny daily-life label."

**中文提示：** 强调 “自己选择” 和 “轻松、可分享”。避免一上来就进入太私人或敏感的标签。可以说：“这三个标签不需要全面定义你，只是今天打开对话的入口。你可以选一个认真一点的、一个日常一点的、一个好玩一点的。”

**If people hesitate / 如果冷场：**

> "It does not need to be deep. You can choose one serious label, one casual label, and one funny label."

> "不用很深刻。可以一个认真一点、一个日常一点、一个好玩一点。比如：产品经理、奶茶人、早起困难户。"

---

## Slide 3 — Warm-Up: Which Shortcut Would You Trust First? (4 min)

**Slide question / 页面问题：**

> "Which shortcut would you trust first: a person's self-description, or a label other people use for them?"

> "你会先相信哪种快捷方式：一个人的自我描述，还是别人给他的标签？"

**What to do / 操作步骤：**

1. Ask everyone to choose quietly.
2. Pair-share for 2 minutes.
3. Invite 2-3 quick responses.

> "Choose one quietly first. No need to explain yet."

> "先在心里选一个，不用马上解释。"

> "Now tell the person next to you: what kind of situation made you choose this answer?"

> "现在告诉旁边的人：是什么样的经历或场景让你这样选？"

**中文提示：** 这一页的重点不是得出答案，而是让大家意识到：我们每天都在使用 “快捷方式”。可以收束说：“自我描述也可能包装自己，别人给的标签也可能带偏见。所以真正有意思的不是选哪边，而是我们什么时候信、为什么信。”

---

## Slide 4 — Shared Inputs: Four Ways Labels Work (6 min)

**What to do / 讲解方式：** 这是信息输入页。每个卡片讲 60-75 秒，重点是给后面讨论提供共同语言。

### 1. Personal — They give language

> "Labels give us language. For example, MBTI gives people a way to talk about preferences and interpersonal differences."

> "标签给我们语言。比如 MBTI 让人更容易谈论偏好、人际差异和工作方式。"

**Host note / 主持提示：** 可以补一句：“有时一个标签会让人觉得：原来我不是奇怪，我只是有一种可以被描述的模式。”

### 2. Social — They connect us to groups

> "Labels also connect us to groups. Social identity theory studies how group membership becomes part of our self-concept."

> "标签也把我们和群体连接起来。社会身份理论研究的是：群体成员身份如何成为自我概念的一部分。"

**Host note / 主持提示：** 举例：老乡、校友、同一行业、同一种 MBTI、同一星座。归属感是真实的，但它也可能创造 “我们” 和 “他们”。

### 3. Ambiguous — They can feel surprisingly accurate

> "Labels can feel surprisingly accurate. In Forer's 1949 study, students rated a vague personality profile as 4.26 out of 5 on average."

> "标签有时会让人觉得特别准。Forer 1949 年的研究里，学生们把一段模糊的性格描述平均打到 4.26 分，满分 5 分。"

**Host note / 主持提示：** 这就是常说的 Barnum Effect / Forer Effect。不要讲太长，只点到为止：“人会主动寻找能对上自己的部分。”

### 4. Playful — They are often entertainment, too

> "Labels are often entertainment, too. A 2024 Pew survey found that 30% of U.S. adults consult astrology, tarot, or fortune tellers at least once a year, often just for fun."

> "标签很多时候也是娱乐。Pew 的调查显示，30% 的美国成年人每年至少一次看星座、塔罗或算命，很多人只是觉得好玩。"

**Closing line / 收束句：**

> "So labels are not only serious identity. They can be language, belonging, self-recognition, and play."

> "所以标签不只是严肃身份。它也可能是语言、归属、自我理解，或者一种游戏。"

## Slide 5 — Useful Language (3 min)

**What to do / 讲解方式：** 不需要逐条细讲。快速带过，提醒大家后面讨论可以借用。

> "Here are some useful words for today. Please use them when you discuss: self-label, assigned label, stereotype, context, and agency."

> "这里有几个今天会用到的词：自我标签、被赋予的标签、刻板印象、语境、选择权。讨论时可以直接用。"

**Useful sentence frames / 可用句型：**

- "In my experience..."
- "It depends on..."
- "That label can be useful when..."
- "It becomes unfair when..."
- "For me, it means..."
- "It does not mean..."

**中文提示：** 如果大家英语水平不一，可以说：“不用追求复杂表达，把自己的经历讲清楚最重要。需要句型时看屏幕。”

---

## Slide 6 — Round 1: Personal Stories First (2 min)

> "Round 1 is about everyday experience. Start small: first meetings, team introductions, family jokes, school memories, dating profiles, hometown comments, online bios."

> "第一轮聊日常经验。可以从很小的场景开始：第一次见面、团队介绍、家庭玩笑、学校记忆、相亲资料、家乡评论、社交主页。"

> "Pick one question first. Each person gives a short example, then the group looks for patterns and differences."

> "先选一个问题。每个人讲一个短例子，然后小组一起找相同点和不同点。"

**中文提示：** 强调 “personal stories first”。这能避免讨论太快变成抽象争论。可以提醒：“不要急着讲道理，先讲一个发生过的场景。”

---

## Slide 7 — Round 1: Four Group Questions (18 min)

**Read all four questions / 先读四个问题：**

1. **What is a label you sometimes use for yourself? In what situation does it feel useful?**
   **你有时会用什么标签描述自己？它在什么场景下有用？**

2. **When has someone guessed something about you from a label? What did they get right or miss?**
   **什么时候别人根据一个标签猜测过你？他们猜对了什么，又错过了什么？**

3. **What is a label people around you often joke about? Why does it feel funny, friendly, or uncomfortable?**
   **你身边有什么标签经常被拿来开玩笑？为什么它会让人觉得好笑、亲近，或者不舒服？**

4. **Which label changes meaning depending on who says it, where they say it, or how well they know you?**
   **哪个标签会因为说的人、场合、亲密程度不同，而意义完全不同？**

**Host instruction / 主持指令：**

> "Choose one question to start. Everyone should answer at least once. After that, you can move to another question."

> "先选一个问题开始。每个人至少回答一次。之后可以换下一个问题。"

**Facilitation / 引导技巧：**

- Q1 最轻松，适合启动讨论。
- Q2 会带出 “别人看到的我”和“真实的我”的差距。
- Q3 注意语气，玩笑有时亲密，有时伤人。
- Q4 是关键问题：同一个标签在不同语境下会变味。
- 如果有人概括某个群体，温和拉回个人经历：“Can you think of a specific example?” / “能不能讲一个具体例子？”
- 如果冷场，主持人先分享：“A label people use for me is ___. It is useful when ___, but it misses ___.”

---

## Slide 8 — Round 1 Extend: Compare the Stories (7 min)

**What to do / 操作步骤：** Round 1 后半段让小组总结，而不是继续散聊。

> "Now compare your stories. Don't just collect examples. Look for patterns, differences, feelings, and context."

> "现在比较你们的故事。不要只是收集例子，试着找模式、差异、感受和语境。"

**Four angles / 四个角度：**

- **Repeating patterns:** Which labels or situations came up more than once?
- **Changing meanings:** Did the same label mean different things in different contexts?
- **Emotional tone:** Did the label feel like pride, relief, pressure, humor, distance, curiosity, or resistance?
- **Try saying:** "For me, it means..."

**中文主持词：**

> "请每组选一个你们发现的 pattern，等下用一句话分享。可以是：我们发现某个标签很常见；或者同一个标签在不同人那里意义完全不同。"

**Report back / 全场回收：** 请 2-3 组每组 30 秒分享一个发现。

**中文提示：** 这一页帮助大家从 “故事” 进入 “反思”。如果时间紧，至少让每组内部总结一句，不一定全场汇报。

---

## Slide 9 — Round 2: Move from Experience to Reflection (2 min)

> "Now we move from experience to reflection. Round 2 has three questions: why it happens, what it does, and what we can do."

> "现在我们从经验进入反思。第二轮有三个问题：它为什么会发生、它带来什么影响、我们可以怎么做。"

> "There is no single answer. Try to use examples from Round 1."

> "没有唯一答案。尽量使用第一轮听到的例子。"

**中文提示：** 如果可以，换组或让大家和新的人讨论。可以说：“找至少两个新面孔，这样你会听到不同故事。”

---

## Slide 10 — Round 2 Q1: Why Do Labels Become So Common? (9 min)

**Question / 问题：**

> "Why do labels become so common?"

> "为什么标签会这么常见？"

**Four explanations on slide / 页面上的四个解释：**

- **Cognitive shortcut:** We group information to reduce complexity and make fast sense of people.
- **Belonging:** Labels can signal “people like me” and make identity easier to share.
- **Social script:** Groups repeat familiar categories: hometown, job, age, MBTI, zodiac, school.
- **Efficiency:** Labels save time in introductions, matching, hiring, dating, and online profiles.

**Group task / 小组任务：**

> "Rank these explanations from most convincing to least convincing. Then add one explanation your group thinks is missing."

> "把这四个解释从最有说服力到最没说服力排序。然后补充一个你们觉得缺失的解释。"

**中文引导提示：**

- 认知捷径：大脑需要分类，否则信息太多。
- 归属感：标签帮我们找到 “同类”。
- 社交脚本：很多场合默认要问职业、年龄、家乡、学校。
- 效率：在线资料、招聘、相亲、团队合作都需要快速判断。

**If discussion gets abstract / 如果讨论太抽象：**

> "Think of one situation: meeting a new colleague, reading a dating profile, interviewing someone, or joining a new group. Which explanation is strongest there?"

> "想一个具体场景：认识新同事、看相亲资料、面试别人、加入新群体。在这个场景里，哪个解释最强？"

---

## Slide 11 — Round 2 Q2: Do Labels Help Us or Limit Us? (9 min)

**Question / 问题：**

> "Do labels help us or limit us?"

> "标签是在帮助我们，还是限制我们？"

**Mini debate / 小辩论：**

> "Split into two sides for 4 minutes. One side argues HELP, one side argues LIMIT. Use examples from Round 1. Then switch sides and make the strongest argument for the opposite view."

> "分成两边辩论 4 分钟。一边主张有帮助，一边主张有限制。用第一轮的例子。然后交换立场，为相反观点做最强论证。"

**HELP side / 有帮助的一面：**

- Labels give language, belonging, confidence, and useful starting points.
- They help people explain preferences, needs, boundaries, and background faster.
- A label can make someone feel seen or understood.

**LIMIT side / 有限制的一面：**

- Labels create assumptions, stereotypes, and frozen first impressions.
- They reduce curiosity and make it harder for people to change.
- A label can become an excuse: “I am just like this.”

**中文提示：** 这一页适合练习 “play devil's advocate”。如果全组都同意某一边，就请他们强制为另一边辩护。目标不是赢，而是看到两边都有真实理由。

**Useful host lines / 主持可用句：**

> "Can both be true in different situations?"

> "在不同场景下，两边会不会都成立？"

> "What kind of label helps? What kind of label limits?"

> "什么样的标签有帮助？什么样的标签会限制人？"

---

## Slide 12 — Round 2 Q3: How Can We Use Labels Without Putting People in a Box? (9 min)

**Question / 问题：**

> "How can we use labels without putting people in a box?"

> "我们怎样使用标签，同时不把人装进盒子里？"

**Four practices / 四个做法：**

1. **Add context**
   > "For me, it means..." / "It helps explain..." / "It does not mean..."
   > “对我来说，它意味着……” / “它帮助解释……” / “它不代表……”

2. **Ask better**
   > "Ask for the story behind the label before assuming the meaning."
   > “先问标签背后的故事，不要直接假设它的含义。”

3. **Make it optional**
   > "Let people choose whether to use a label, explain it, skip it, or change it."
   > “让人可以选择使用、解释、跳过或改变一个标签。”

4. **Keep updating**
   > "Leave room for people to grow, contradict themselves, or surprise you later."
   > “给别人留出成长、变化、自相矛盾、后来让你惊讶的空间。”

**Group challenge / 小组挑战：**

> "Create two useful sentences: one for introducing yourself, and one for responding to someone else's label."

> "请小组创造两个实用句子：一句用于介绍自己，一句用于回应别人的标签。"

**Examples / 示例：**

- "I sometimes call myself ___, but for me it mainly means ___."
- "That's interesting. What does that label mean for you?"
- "People often describe me as ___, but it does not mean ___."
- "I can see why that label is useful, but I would like to know the story behind it."

**中文提示：** 这一页要落到行动。可以强调：“目标不是停止贴标签，而是更有意识地使用标签。标签可以是入口，不要让它变成终点。”

---

## Slide 13 — Open Questions: Flip Cards (10 min)

**What to do / 操作步骤：** 6 张卡片，每张一个开放问题。点击卡片翻开，邀请一位志愿者回答，再让小组延伸讨论 1 分钟。

> "Now let's open some new conversations. Pick a sticker, flip the card, and answer honestly."

> "现在我们用卡片开启一些新对话。选一个图案，翻开，然后诚实回答。"

**Cards / 卡片问题：**

1. **Puzzle**
   > "What is one small thing people often misunderstand about you?"
   > “别人常常误解你的一个小地方是什么？”

2. **Playlist**
   > "What kind of music fits your current mood this week?"
   > “什么音乐最符合你这周的状态？”

3. **Map**
   > "What place has shaped the way you talk, think, or relax?"
   > “哪个地方影响了你说话、思考或放松的方式？”

4. **Clock**
   > "What is something you used to care about more than you do now?"
   > “有什么事你以前更在意，现在没那么在意了？”

5. **Pass**
   > "What is a question you wish people asked more often?"
   > “你希望别人更常问你的一个问题是什么？”

6. **Compass**
   > "What kind of person do you want to become more like?"
   > “你希望自己更接近哪种人？”

**If no touchscreen / 如果没有触屏：** 主持人随机点卡片，读出中英文问题，请志愿者回答。回答后可以让这位志愿者指定下一张。

**中文提示：** 这些问题比前面更开放，不全是直接谈标签，但都在把人从 “一个标签” 展开成 “一个故事”。可以说：“这些问题帮助我们看到标签之外的东西。”

---

## Slide 14 — Closing: I Contain Multitudes (3 min)

**Quote / 引语：**

> "I contain multitudes."

> "我包含着多重自我。"

**Source / 来源：** Walt Whitman, *Song of Myself*, 1855.

**Closing message / 收尾主持词：**

> "A label can start a conversation. It does not have to finish one."

> "一个标签可以开启理解，但不必终结理解。"

> "Today we talked about labels as shortcuts, language, belonging, jokes, assumptions, and stories. Maybe the key is not to throw away all labels, but to hold them lightly."

> "今天我们把标签看作快捷方式、语言、归属、玩笑、假设和故事。也许关键不是扔掉所有标签，而是不要把它们抓得太紧。"

> "Thank you for sharing your stories. See you next time."

> "谢谢大家今天分享自己的故事。我们下次见。"

**中文提示：** 念完 “A label can start a conversation...” 后停顿 2 秒。不要总结太满，留一点余味。

## Facilitation Cheat Sheet / 引导小抄

| Situation 情况 | Response 回应 |
|-----------|----------|
| 冷场 silence | Share your own example first: "A label people use for me is ___, and it misses ___." / 先讲自己的例子。 |
| 一人独占 one person dominates | "Let's hear from someone who hasn't spoken yet." / “我们听听还没发言的朋友。” |
| 讨论太抽象 too abstract | "Can you connect that to one real situation?" / “能不能连接到一个真实场景？” |
