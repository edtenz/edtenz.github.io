# Host Script: Consumerism

> **消费主义：我们到底在买什么？**
> 90 min · 2 rounds · 7 questions · 8 open cards

---

## Key Vocabulary / 核心词汇表

| English | Pronunciation | 中文 | English Definition | 中文解释 |
|---------|--------------|------|-------------------|----------|
| Consumerism | /kənˈsjuːmərɪzəm/ | 消费主义 | A way of life where buying serves identity, status, and belonging — not just need | 购买不只是为了使用，更是为了身份、地位和归属 |
| Impulse buying | /ˈɪmpʌls ˈbaɪɪŋ/ | 冲动消费 | Buying without planning, driven by emotion, pressure, or discounts | 没有计划、被情绪或折扣驱动的购买 |

## Pre-Session Checklist / 开场前准备

- [ ] Test slides and projector
- [ ] Arrange seats in groups of 5-7
- [ ] Prepare timer
- [ ] Arrive early, greet people at the door

---

## Slide 1 — Title (1 min)

> "Welcome! Tonight: **Consumerism** — Buying Needs, Buying Identity, Buying Anxiety."
> "消费主义——我们到底在买什么？"

**中文提示：** 轻松开场，可以说"今晚我们聊聊花钱这件事——不是教你省钱。"

---

## Slide 2 — Ice Breaker (5 min)

> "Pair up. Share: your name, what you do, and the last thing you bought online. Was it worth it or not?"

**Demo:** "I'm Eddie. The last thing I bought was ___. Honestly, it was / wasn't worth it because ___."

---

## Slide 3 — Warm-up (3 min)

**What to do:** Show "The Need Receipt" vs "The Mood Receipt." Ask the question.

> "Look at these two receipts. One solves a problem. The other changes your mood."

> "Quick pair-share: your last purchase — did it solve a problem, express who you are, or change your mood?"

---

## Slide 4 — Three Concepts (3 min)

1. **Consumerism** (消费主义) — "Buying is not just about use — it's about identity, status, reward, and belonging."
2. **Impulse Buying** (冲动消费) — "You buy quickly because of emotion, social pressure, discounts, or FOMO."
3. **Minimalism** (极简生活) — "Owning fewer things on purpose, so your resources serve what matters."

> "Core question: **Are we satisfying needs, or manufacturing new desires?**"

---

## Slide 5 — Stats (2 min)

> - "**15.97 trillion RMB** — online retail sales in China, 2025."
> - "**26.1%** of China's retail is physical goods sold online."
> - "**$807 billion** — China's livestream e-commerce market."
> - "**833 million** livestreaming users in China."

> "When the mall is in your pocket, the checkout counter never closes."

---

## Slide 6 — Four Perspectives (2 min)

> 1. "**Convenience** — easier than ever to compare, buy, access."
> 2. "**Persuasion** — livestream hosts, countdown timers, and algorithms turn shopping into entertainment."
> 3. "**Emotional Value** — 56.3% of Gen Z are willing to pay for happiness."
> 4. "**Environmental Cost** — 92 million tonnes of textile waste every year."

---

## Slide 7 — Round 1 Title (30 sec)

> "Round 1: **Recognizing Your Own Habits.** 25 minutes, 4 questions. Be honest — no judgment."

---

## Slide 8 — Round 1 Questions (25 min)

- **Q1 The Last Purchase Test:** "Last non-essential thing you bought — what triggered it: need, discount, mood, boredom, or social pressure?"
- **Q2 Online vs. Real Stores:** "Where do you buy things you didn't plan to buy? Taobao, JD, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, or offline?"
- **Q3 Brands and Identity:** "Is there a brand or product that says something about who you are? Real expression, or performance?"
- **Q4 Regret and Satisfaction:** "What purchase gave you long-term satisfaction? What quickly became clutter? What's the difference?"

**Facilitation:**
- This topic is light and fun at first — let people laugh about impulse buys.
- Go deeper with Q3 and Q4.
- 冷场: "Let me start — my worst impulse buy was ___. What about you?"

---

## Slide 9 — Useful Expressions (shown during Round 1)

> "'I bought it because ___', 'I was influenced by ___', 'The hidden cost is ___', 'I think brands sell us ___, not just products.'"

---

## Slide 10 — Transition (2 min)

> "Three forces behind every purchase:"
> 1. "**Need** — it solves a real problem."
> 2. "**Desire** — it gives pleasure, identity, novelty, or status."
> 3. "**Anxiety** — it promises: buy this before you fall behind."

> "Round 2 is not about judging. It's about seeing the system more clearly."

---

## Slide 11 — Round 2 Title (30 sec)

> "Switch groups. Round 2: **Seeing The System.** 3 questions."

---

## Slide 12 — Round 2 Q1: Freedom or Manipulation? (8 min)

> "**Is consumerism mostly freedom or manipulation?**"
> - "FREEDOM: People choose what makes life better. Shopping supports creativity and self-expression."
> - "MANIPULATION: Algorithms learn our weak moments. Brands sell identity, not function."

> "Discussion tip: **defend the side you disagree with** for two minutes."

---

## Slide 13 — Round 2 Q2: Role-Play (8 min)

> "**Who benefits from our desire?** Split into 4 groups:"
> - "**Platforms** (Taobao, Douyin) — Should they reduce manipulative design?"
> - "**Brands & Influencers** — Is emotional selling a problem or good communication?"
> - "**Government** — Should there be stronger rules on ads and dark patterns?"
> - "**Each of Us** — How much responsibility should individuals take?"

> "5 min prep, then cross-debate."

---

## Slide 14 — Round 2 Q3: Healthier Relationship (9 min)

> "**What would a healthier relationship with buying look like?**"
> - "Wait 24 hours before non-essential purchases."
> - "Ask: 'What problem will this still solve one month from now?'"
> - "Unfollow one account that makes you feel inadequate."
> - "Track one week of small purchases without judgment."
> - "Repair, borrow, or buy second-hand once this month."

> "**Finish with: 'This week, I will ___.'"**

---

## Slide 15 — Eight Open Cards (10 min)

> "Eight cards, eight questions. Tap, answer honestly, invite someone else."

---

## Slide 16 — Closing Quote (2 min)

> "'Too many people spend money they haven't earned, to buy things they don't want, to impress people they don't like.' — Will Rogers"

> "Thanks for speaking, listening, and noticing what usually stays invisible."
> "感谢你今晚的真诚分享。"

## Facilitation Cheat Sheet

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| 冷场 | Share your own worst impulse buy. Humor unlocks honesty. |
| 一人独占 | "Love that! Who has a totally different spending style?" |
| 变成说教 | "Let's keep it personal — what's YOUR experience?" |
| 跑题 | "Interesting — how does that connect to how we consume?" |
| 能量下降 | Quick show of hands: "Who bought something THIS WEEK they didn't plan?" |
| 超时 | Skip Q2 in Round 1. Protect Round 2 Q3. |
