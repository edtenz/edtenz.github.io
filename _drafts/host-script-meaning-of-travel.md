# Host Script: Meaning of Travel

> **旅行的意义：是在放松自己，还是在完成一种社交展示？**
> 90 min · 2 rounds · 7 questions · 8 blind box cards

---

## Key Vocabulary / 核心词汇表

| English | Pronunciation | 中文 | English Definition | 中文解释 |
|---------|--------------|------|-------------------|----------|
| Check-in Tourism | — | 打卡式旅行 | Visiting a spot just to take a photo and prove you were there | 到了景点拍照证明去过就走 |

| Slow Travel | — | 深度旅行/慢旅行 | Staying longer in one place to deeply experience local life | 在一个地方待更久，深入体验当地生活 |

## Pre-Session Checklist / 开场前准备

- [ ] Test slides and projector
- [ ] Arrange seats in groups of 5-7
- [ ] Prepare timer
- [ ] Arrive early, greet people

---

## Slide 1 — Title (1 min)

> "Welcome! Tonight: **What's the Point of Travel?** — Check-in Tourism, Slow Travel, Solo Trips & The Instagram Trap."
> "旅行的意义——是在放松自己，还是在完成一种社交展示？"

**中文提示：** 旅行话题轻松有趣，大家都有话说。开场保持活泼。

---

## Slide 2 — Ice Breaker (5 min)

> "Pair up. Share: your name, what you do, and the last place you traveled to — and why you went."

**Demo:** "I'm Eddie. My last trip was to ___. I went because ___."

---

## Slide 3 — Warm-up: Quick Poll (2 min)

**What to do:** Read each line, ask for raised hands, react to the results.

> "Raise your hand if..."
> - "You've visited 3+ cities in a single weekend." (特种兵！)
> - "You've taken a photo of food before eating it on a trip."
> - "You traveled somewhere mainly because you saw it on Xiaohongshu."
> - "You felt MORE tired after a vacation than before."
> - "You've traveled alone — and liked it."

> "We all travel — but clearly not the same way. Let's talk about why."

---

## Slide 4 — Three Concepts (3 min)

1. **Check-in Tourism** (打卡式旅行) — "See it, snap it, leave. The destination is content, not experience."
2. **Speed Tourism** (特种兵旅游) — "Maximum places, minimum time. Travel as endurance sport."
3. **Slow Travel** (深度旅行) — "Stay longer, see less, feel more. Travel as transformation."

> "No judgment — all three are valid. But today we ask: **what are you really looking for when you travel?**"

---

## Slide 5 — Stats (2 min)

> - "**6.1 billion** domestic trips in China in 2024."
> - "**73%** of Gen-Z say they travel 'for content' — photos/videos to post."
> - "**¥326** average daily spend for 特种兵 travelers."
> - "**61%** of travelers say their trip 'looked better online than it felt.'"

> "We're traveling more than ever — but are we EXPERIENCING more?"

---

## Slide 6 — The Instagram Trap (2 min)

**What to do:** Share the USC study finding. Make it hit home.

> "A 2023 study from USC found: when you photograph an experience, your enjoyment drops about 15%. You remember what it LOOKED like, not how it FELT."

> "When you just experience it — you're more present, form richer memories, report higher satisfaction."

> "The camera becomes a wall between you and the moment. But — should we stop taking photos?"

---

## Slide 7 — Round 1 Title (30 sec)

> "Round 1: **Your Travel Habits.** 25 minutes, 4 questions. Share real experiences."

---

## Slide 8 — Round 1 Questions (25 min)

- **Q1 Your Travel Style:** "Are you more of a 特种兵, a 打卡 traveler, or a slow traveler? Give an example."
- **Q2 The Phone Test:** "On your last trip, how much time did you spend taking photos vs. being present?"
- **Q3 Solo vs. Together:** "Have you traveled alone? What surprised you? If not — what stops you?"
- **Q4 "Was It Worth It?":** "Have you arrived at a famous spot and felt... disappointed? What did you do?"

**Facilitation:**
- This topic is fun — energy should be natural.
- Q4 often gets great stories. Let it run.
- 冷场: "My worst tourist disappointment was ___. Anyone relate?"
- If someone says they only do 特种兵 travel, don't judge — ask what they get from it.

---

## Slide 9 — Useful Expressions

> "'I'm the kind of traveler who ___', 'For me, the best part of a trip is ___', 'Honestly, I've caught myself ___', 'I used to think ___, but now I feel ___'"

---

## Slide 10 — Bigger Picture / Before Round 2 (2 min)

> "How we travel reveals what we value:"
> 1. "**Experience Economy** — we spend on experiences, then reduce them to shareable content."
> 2. "**FOMO Tourism** — 'everyone's been there, so I have to go too.'"
> 3. "**Burnout Escapes** — we escape 996 culture but fill trips with the same productivity mindset."

---

## Slide 11 — Round 2 Title (30 sec)

> "Switch groups. Round 2: **The Deeper Questions.** 3 questions."

---

## Slide 12 — Round 2 Q1: Relaxation or Performance? (8 min)

> "**Be honest: when you travel, are you doing it for yourself — or partly for your audience?**"
> - "POSTING IS FINE: Sharing is natural. Photos help you remember. You can enjoy AND photograph."
> - "IT CHANGES THINGS: You choose destinations for 'shareability.' The trip becomes labor — angles, captions, edits."

> "**Key question: If social media disappeared tomorrow, would your travel style change?**"

---

## Slide 13 — Round 2 Q2: What Shapes Travel? (8 min)

> "**What shapes how we travel?** 4 forces:"
> - "**Social Media** — platforms reward photogenic moments over meaningful ones."
> - "**Work Culture** — 3 days off = speed tourism. Overwork forces shallow travel."
> - "**Peer Pressure** — travel as social currency. When did it become competitive?"
> - "**Inner Needs** — escape, growth, curiosity. What's the emotional engine?"

> "Which force drives YOU most?"

---

## Slide 14 — Round 2 Q3: Intentional Travel (9 min)

> "**Design your perfect next trip based on what you actually NEED:**"
> - "Would you go alone or with someone?"
> - "Would you plan every hour or leave it open?"
> - "Would you post about it or keep it private?"
> - "What would you want to FEEL when you come home?"
> - "What's one thing you'd do differently from your last trip?"

> "**Share: 'My next trip, I want to ___.'"**

---

## Slide 15 — Blind Box Questions (10 min)

> "Eight boxes, eight questions. Tap one, answer, invite someone else."

---

## Slide 16 — Closing Quote (2 min)

> "'We travel not to escape life, but for life not to escape us.'"

> "Wherever you go next — go for yourself first."
> "无论下一次去哪里，先为自己出发。"

## Facilitation Cheat Sheet

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| 冷场 | Share your own travel embarrassment. Everyone has one. |
| 一人独占 | "Great trip! Who's had a completely opposite experience?" |
| 只聊攻略 | "Beyond logistics — how did the trip make you FEEL?" |
| 跑题 | "Fun story! How does that connect to why we travel?" |
| 能量下降 | Quick: "Raise hand — 特种兵 or 慢旅行? Why?" |
| 超时 | Skip Q2 in Round 1. Protect Round 2 Q3. |
