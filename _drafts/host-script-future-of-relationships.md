# Host Script: The Future of Relationships

> **未来的人际关系：科技让我们更近，还是更远？**
> 90 min · 2 rounds · 7 questions · 9 open cards

## Pre-Session Checklist / 开场前准备

- [ ] Test slides and projector
- [ ] Arrange seats in groups of 5–7
- [ ] Prepare timer
- [ ] Arrive early, greet people at the door

---

## Slide 1 — Title (1 min)

> "Welcome! Tonight we're talking about **The Future of Relationships** — how technology is changing the way we connect, date, make friends, and even feel less alone."
> "未来的人际关系——科技让我们更近，还是更远？"

**中文提示：** 轻松开场："今晚我们聊一个每个人都在经历的变化——我们交朋友、谈恋爱、甚至排解孤独的方式，正在被科技彻底改变。"

---

## Slide 2 — Ice Breaker (5 min)

> "Pair up. Share: your name, what you do, and how you usually meet new people — apps, work, friends, hobbies, or events?"

**Demo:** "I'm Eddie. I usually meet new people through ___. The last new person I connected with was ___ — we met because ___."

**中文提示：** 强调"你上一次认识一个新朋友是什么时候？通过什么方式？"——这个问题让大家立刻意识到社交习惯的变化。

---

## Slide 3 — Warm-up: Open Your Phone (3 min)

> "Quick challenge! Open your WeChat right now and count three numbers:"
> 1. "**Total contacts** — how many people are in your list?"
> 2. "**Last 7 days** — how many did you actually talk to this week?"
> 3. "**Real talk** — how many could you call at midnight if you felt terrible?"

> "Share your three numbers with the person next to you. **The gap is the story.**"

**中文提示：** 这个环节很容易操作——大家直接拿手机看。数字出来后自然会笑、会惊讶。追问："从第一个数到第三个数，中间那些人去哪了？"

---

## Slide 4 — Three Concepts (3 min)

1. **Digital Intimacy** (数字亲密) — "Forming real emotional bonds through screens — without ever being in the same room."
2. **Activity Partners / 搭子文化** — "Low-cost, low-commitment connections built around a shared activity. Efficient, but not deep."
3. **AI Companionship** (AI 陪伴) — "Using AI for emotional support. Always available, never judgmental. But is it real connection?"

> "Core question: **Will technology make relationships easier to start, or harder to deepen?**"

---

## Slide 5 — Stats (2 min)

> - "**1.09 billion** internet users in China — nearly everyone is online."
> - "**300%** growth in '搭子' posts on Xiaohongshu in one year."
> - "**20 million+** people use Character.ai monthly — talking to AI for companionship."
> - "**46%** of Gen Z say they feel lonely most or all of the time."

> "More tools for connection than any generation in history — yet loneliness is rising. What is missing?"

---

## Slide 6 — Four Perspectives (2 min)

> 1. "**Convenience vs. Depth** — Apps make it easy to find people. But swiping and matching rarely lead to deep connection."
> 2. "**The 搭子 Economy** — Function-based bonds are efficient. But where does intimacy live?"
> 3. "**AI That Listens** — AI never judges, never leaves. For some it fills a gap. For others it removes motivation to connect with humans."
> 4. "**Remote Everything** — Modern life is designed so you never NEED to see another person. Convenience becomes isolation."

---

## Slide 7 — Round 1 Title (30 sec)

> "Round 1: **How We Connect Now.** 25 minutes, 4 questions. Start from your own experience."

---

## Slide 8 — Round 1 Questions (25 min)

- **Q1 The New Friend Test:** "How did you meet your most recent new friend? App, event, work, mutual friend, or chance? How long did it take to feel real?"
- **Q2 搭子: Friend or Function?** "Do you have a 搭子? Is that friendship or just convenience? Can it become deeper?"
- **Q3 Closer Online?** "Have you felt closer to someone online than to people you see daily? What made it feel real — or was it an illusion?"
- **Q4 The Low-Cost Default:** "Sending memes, liking posts, voice messages — is this 'maintaining a relationship' or just the appearance of one?"

**Facilitation:**
- Q1 is universal and easy. Let everyone share.
- Q2 will resonate with younger participants — let them explain 搭子 to those unfamiliar.
- Q3 might surprise people. Encourage specifics.
- Q4 is slightly confrontational — acknowledge: "We all do this. The question is whether it's enough."
- 冷场: "I'll start — I send voice messages to friends I haven't seen in months. Does that count as a real relationship? I'm not sure."

---

## Slide 9 — Useful Expressions (shown during Round 1)

> "'I mostly connect through ___', 'It feels real / shallow because ___', 'Technology helps us ___ but makes it harder to ___', 'I think we are trading ___ for ___'"

---

## Slide 10 — Transition (2 min)

> "Three things technology optimizes for:"
> 1. "**Efficiency** — find people faster. But deep bonds grow slowly."
> 2. "**Safety** — control who you talk to. But avoiding risk means avoiding vulnerability."
> 3. "**Optionality** — infinite choices. But abundance makes people feel replaceable."

> "Round 2 asks: what are we willing to trade?"

---

## Slide 11 — Round 2 Title (30 sec)

> "Switch groups. Round 2: **What Are We Trading?** 3 questions. Go from personal to societal."

---

## Slide 12 — Round 2 Q1: AI Companionship (8 min)

> "**Is AI companionship helping or replacing human connection?**"
> - "IT HELPS: Safe space to practice openness. Available 24/7. Supplements human relationships."
> - "IT REPLACES: Removes motivation to tolerate human imperfection. Illusion of intimacy. The easier the substitute, the less we invest in the real thing."

> "Would you feel comfortable if your best friend said their closest relationship was with an AI?"

**中文提示：** 这个话题可能两极分化。有人觉得AI陪伴很好，有人觉得可怕。两边都要让说话。追问："你自己会用AI聊天来排解孤独吗？"

---

## Slide 13 — Round 2 Q2: 搭子 Culture (8 min)

> "**Is 搭子 culture making socializing healthier or shallower?**"
> - "HEALTHIER: Low pressure, clear expectations, boundaries built in."
> - "SHALLOWER: Disposable connections, people become interchangeable, no vulnerability required."

> "Is 搭子 a stepping stone to deeper friendship — or a substitute for it?"

**中文提示：** 可以追问："你的搭子有没有变成真正的朋友？如果没有——为什么？"让大家反思功能性关系的极限。

---

## Slide 14 — Round 2 Q3: 10 Years From Now (9 min)

> "**What will relationships look like in 10 years?**"
> - "Will people choose AI over imperfect human partners?"
> - "Will 搭子 become the default model?"
> - "Will in-person time become a luxury?"
> - "Will loneliness become a public health crisis?"
> - "Will we develop new social skills — or lose the old ones?"

> "**Finish with: 'In 10 years, I hope my relationships will ___.'"**

**中文提示：** 这是一个开放性展望。鼓励大家诚实而不是乐观。可以先说："我其实有点担心___。但我希望___。"给出两面的空间。

---

## Slide 15 — Nine Open Cards (10 min)

> "Nine cards, nine questions. Tap, answer honestly, invite someone else."

**中文提示：** 这一轮问题大多与主题无直接关联，增加趣味性。可以说"最后一轮是开盲盒——海洋动物卡片，翻到什么聊什么，放松一下。"

---

## Slide 16 — Closing Quote (2 min)

> "'We expect more from technology and less from each other.' — Sherry Turkle"

> "The future of relationships is not decided by technology. It is decided by what we choose to protect."
> "感谢你今晚的真诚分享。愿你找到值得投入的关系。"

**中文提示：** 结尾简短有力。可以加一句："今晚回去，放下手机，给一个重要的人打个电话——不是发消息，是打电话。"

## Facilitation Cheat Sheet

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| 冷场 | Share your own screen time or social habits: "I spend ___ hours on WeChat daily. Is that connection or addiction?" |
| 一人独占 | "Great point! Who has a completely different experience with online socializing?" |
| 变成说教 | "Let's keep it personal — what does YOUR social life actually look like?" |
| 跑题 | "Interesting — how does that connect to how technology shapes our relationships?" |
| AI话题太抽象 | Ground it: "Has anyone here actually talked to an AI chatbot? What was it like?" |
| 搭子概念不清楚 | Ask younger members: "Can someone explain 搭子 to us? Give a real example." |
| 能量下降 | Quick poll: "Raise your hand if you have more than 500 WeChat contacts. Now raise your hand if you talked to more than 5 of them this week." |
| 超时 | Skip Q4 in Round 1. Protect Round 2 Q3 — the future scenario is the emotional peak. |
